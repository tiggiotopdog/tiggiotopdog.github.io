River Bend Stories: Three Complete Short Speculative Tales

A collection of three original, self-contained speculative stories about ordinary people in a small river town, each with a complete ending.

River Bend Stories is a collection of three original short stories set in a small town where ordinary days sometimes bend in unexpected ways. Each story is complete on its own and resolves its central question, so you can read one, several, or all three in a single sitting.

The characters are neighbors, volunteers, and families who depend on one another. Their problems are personal and practical: a misplaced promise, a shared responsibility, a choice about who to trust. The speculative elements add curiosity and wonder, but the focus stays on the people and the decisions they make.

The collection suits readers who enjoy character-driven fiction with a touch of the strange. It also works well for small groups such as volunteer teams, book clubs, and nonprofit staff who want a short, discussable read for a break room, a retreat, or a community gathering. Each ending leaves room for conversation about what the characters chose and why.

These are original stories written for this collection. They are not fan fiction and do not imitate any living author's work.
