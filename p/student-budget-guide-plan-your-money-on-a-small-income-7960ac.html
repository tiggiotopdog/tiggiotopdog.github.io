Student Budget Guide: Plan Your Money on a Small Income

A practical written guide with worksheet pages that helps students plan a monthly budget, track everyday spending, and set aside a small amount for savings.

Many students have an irregular income from part-time jobs, family support, or loans, and bills still arrive on fixed dates. This guide is for anyone who wants a clear, simple way to see where their money goes each month.

The guide walks through five steps: listing your income, sorting fixed and flexible expenses, setting realistic spending limits, tracking purchases for one month, and adjusting the plan at month's end. Each step explains what to do and why it matters, using plain language and examples from typical student budgets.

Included are worksheet pages you can write on by hand or fill in on screen. They cover a monthly plan, a weekly spending log, a list of recurring subscriptions, and a simple savings goal tracker. The pages are laid out to print cleanly in black and white.

This guide does not promise that you will save a set amount or pay off debt. It gives you a structure to make decisions based on your own numbers, and the results depend on your income, costs, and habits.

It suits students in high school, college, or trade programs, and works equally well for a first budget or for rebuilding a plan that stopped working.
