Shop Reorder Worksheets: Count Stock, Set Reorder Points, Plan Orders

A set of worksheet pages and a short written guide that help small independent shops count stock, set reorder points, and decide what to order next.

Running a small shop means balancing bestsellers that sell out with slow items that tie up cash and shelf space. Without a regular routine, reordering tends to happen in a rush, often after something has already run out.

This set includes a weekly stock count sheet, a reorder point worksheet, and a one-page order planner. Each page is laid out to be printed and kept at the back counter or in a binder, so you can use it with pen and paper alongside whatever records you already keep.

The short guide walks through each page. It shows how to estimate weekly sales from your own sales history, how to add a safety margin for items with unpredictable supplier delivery times, and how to spot slow movers before they take up more room than they earn.

Working through the pages once a week for a month will give you a clearer picture of your own numbers. How useful this is depends on how consistently you count and how accurate your existing records are. It organizes the information you already have; it does not predict future sales.

This set is best suited to independent shops with a limited product range. Larger operations with dedicated inventory systems may find less here that is new to them.
