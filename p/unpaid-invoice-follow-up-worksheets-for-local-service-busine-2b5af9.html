Unpaid Invoice Follow-Up Worksheets for Local Service Businesses

A set of printable worksheet pages and polite reminder wording to help small local service businesses track overdue invoices and follow up on them consistently.

If you run a cleaning, landscaping, repair, or other local service business, you may have finished jobs where the customer still hasn't paid. Following up can feel awkward, and without a simple system it's easy to forget who owes what, when you last asked, and what to say next.

This is a text page you read inside Gumroad and can print from your browser. It includes an overdue invoice tracking worksheet with space for the customer, job date, amount, due date, and each follow-up. It also includes a step-by-step reminder schedule you can adapt, and short example wording for a friendly first reminder, a firmer second reminder, and a final notice. The wording works for a text, an email, or a phone call.

There is also a one-page checklist for setting clearer payment terms before future jobs. It covers stating the due date up front, listing the payment methods you accept, and confirming the agreed price in writing.

These pages are a practical starting point, not legal or financial advice. Rules on late fees, interest, and debt collection vary by location, so check what applies to you before adding penalties to your terms. The pages can't make a customer pay, but they can help you follow up in a calmer and more organized way.

You can print as many copies of the worksheet pages as your business needs and adjust the example wording to fit your own voice and customers.
